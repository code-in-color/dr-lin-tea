Hibiscus Fizz

Tart hibiscus, green tea, and lotus flower come together in a crisp sparkling tea with a naturally vibrant, fruit-forward character.

Made with only five ingredients and lightly sweetened with turbinado sugar, Hibiscus Fizz offers a refreshing alternative to soda that is cleaner and intentionally crafted.

5 ingredients · Real botanicals · Nothing artificial

Only Five Ingredients

01

Water

Pure sparkling water forms the base.

02

Hibiscus

Known for its deep ruby color and naturally tart, fruit-like flavor.

03

Green Tea

Provides structure, gentle caffeine, and classic tea character.

04

Lotus Flower

A botanical used in traditional herbal practices that adds delicate floral complexity.

05

Turbinado Sugar

Lightly processed cane sugar that preserves natural molasses notes.

Why We Made This

We wanted to create a drink that brought together centuries of botanical tradition with modern taste. Hibiscus Fizz isn't trying to be anything other than what it is: a thoughtfully crafted sparkling tea made from real ingredients you can recognize.

No artificial flavors. No lengthy ingredient lists. Just five simple components that work together to create something genuinely refreshing.

We're building Dr. Lin Tea for people who care about what goes into their body, but don't want to sacrifice taste or enjoyment.
