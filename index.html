Sparkling Botanical Tea

Ruby Fizz

Tart hibiscus, premium loose-leaf tea, and blue lotus flower come together in a crisp sparkling tea with a naturally vibrant, fruit-forward character.

Made with minimal ingredients and lightly sweetened with turbinado sugar, Ruby Fizz offers a refreshing alternative to soda that is cleaner and intentionally crafted.

Minimal ingredients · Real botanicals · Nothing artificial

Why We Made This

We wanted to create a drink that brought together centuries of botanical tradition with modern taste. Hibiscus Fizz isn't trying to be anything other than what it is: a thoughtfully crafted sparkling tea made from real ingredients you can recognize.

No artificial flavors. No lengthy ingredient lists. Just minimal ingredients that work together to create something genuinely refreshing.

We're building Dr. Lin Tea for people who care about what goes into their body, but don't want to sacrifice taste or enjoyment.
